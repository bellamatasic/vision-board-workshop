How the evening goes

Part reflection, part craft night, part catching up with women who also want next year to feel different. Cameras on if you like, pajamas welcome.

  1. Look back at 2026

    A few guided prompts on what worked, what drained you, and what you're proud of.

    ~15 min

  2. Picture 2027

    Name how you want the year to feel, then the few things that would make it so.

    ~15 min

  3. Build your board

    Cut, paste, or drag images together while we talk. Paper or digital, your call.

    ~30 min

  4. Share one intention

    Say one thing out loud to the group. It's easier to keep a promise someone heard.

    ~10 min

Make it a real board

A board you can hang on your wall and walk past every morning hits differently than a file on your phone. So I'm asking you to go physical if you can. I'll send reminders to help you get your supplies ready in time.

Recommended

Physical board

  • A corkboard, foam board, or poster board (around 16×20 in. works well)
  • 15–25 printed photos of places, people, and moments you want in 2027
  • Magazines to cut up, if you have any
  • Scissors, a glue stick or pushpins, and a marker for words

Order your prints a week ahead from a drugstore photo counter or an online print service, or print them at home.

Can't get a board in time?

  • Make a Canva board or a Pinterest board before the evening
  • Bring it along and add to it with us
  • Print it later and hang it up
